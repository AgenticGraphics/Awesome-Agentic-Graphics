THE SURVEY

Graphics, with
agency.

Computer graphics is built on code. Language-model agents can now create that code, operate graphics tools, and refine their work through feedback.

As research expands across graphics tasks, shared ideas are often hidden behind different terminology. This survey brings them together through two contributions: a unified taxonomy and an agentic literature survey system.
