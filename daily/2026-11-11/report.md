### [ManimCode: Learning Visual Self-Correction for Educational Animation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08294)
core | High | new_relative_to_snapshot
Application: 2D Graphics, Dynamic / Animation and temporal scene; operations: Generation
Learns the complete animation correction loop in a 4B model from 24,034 quality-filtered trajectories, improving render success to 96.6%/97.0% on two benchmarks.
Scope: A trained multimodal controller generates and rewrites executable Manim animation programs based on rendered-frame critiques and execution/layout diagnostics.
Limits: Quality gains are smaller than executability gains; revisions can oscillate and leave clipped objects. Human validation uses one blinded team member on 98 pairs; automatic judge agreement is weak for small score gaps. Long-form results use substituted judges and are not directly comparable to published benchmark scores. No dedicated limitations section; these limitations were inspected in Sections 5.4 and Appendix C.
